# Legal / compliance source notes

For the client's legal adviser and future maintainers. These are reference points, not a substitute for professional review.

## Company information
- Italian Civil Code, art. 2250 — information companies must expose in correspondence and electronic communications, as applicable.

## Consumer / used-goods guarantee
- Ministero delle Imprese e del Made in Italy — **Garanzia legale**: https://www.mimit.gov.it/it/mercato-e-consumatori/tutela-del-consumatore/diritti-del-consumatore/garanzia-legale
- Ministero delle Imprese e del Made in Italy — **Garanzia commerciale (o convenzionale)**: https://www.mimit.gov.it/it/mercato-e-consumatori/tutela-del-consumatore/diritti-del-consumatore/garanzia-commerciale
- Codice del Consumo, including the provisions on conformity guarantees for consumer goods.

## Privacy / cookies
- Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), especially arts. 5, 6, 13 and data-subject rights.
- Garante per la protezione dei dati personali — Cookie FAQ: https://www.garanteprivacy.it/faq/cookie

## Project-specific compliance posture
- No online vehicle purchase is concluded on this website.
- No analytics, ad pixels or profiling cookies are shipped.
- No remote fonts, map embeds, video embeds or chat widgets are shipped.
- The form is used only for direct enquiries and does not subscribe users to marketing.
- If any of those facts change, review the legal pages and consent requirements before enabling the new feature.
